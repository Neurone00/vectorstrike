The capture is an engineering success, not an art pass: bare ribbon road, plain sky, placeholder vehicle, standard-looking lighting and debug HUD. It should not be judged against the finished concepts yet.

## Implementation mismatches Claude should resolve

1. **Renderer:** agreed plan says Unity + URP; `GraphicsSettings.asset` currently has no custom render pipeline. Shader and bloom look matching should happen after Claude confirms the target pipeline. The art direction does not authorize ChatGPT to migrate it.
2. **Track geometry:** the scene uses a variable-curvature Catmull–Rom ribbon. Fixed R64/30° art modules cannot be assumed to tile the whole existing route exactly. Adapt dressing to the spline or explicitly revise the route with the user. Keep collision/route logic with Claude.
3. **Circuit length and straight:** the existing route is 1366.07m at the current sampling, rather than ~1.5km. It has no true long straight. The new art map records reality instead of silently redesigning it.
4. **Status records:** older documents still said there was no engine implementation. Current-date notes now distinguish existing engineering content from missing production art.
